METRI FORMULA ENGINE (COMPACT)
- Formulas are row-by-row scalar calculations in Rust (measures).
- Aggregations are applied at the end (metrics: AVG, SUM, COUNT, etc.).
- GOLDEN RULE: NEVER use aggregations (AVG, SUM) inside formulas. Use measures for the formula and metrics for the aggregation referencing the alias.
- Safe division: `numerator / NULLIF(denominator, 0)`.
- Valid functions: ABS, ROUND, CEIL, FLOOR, POWER, SQRT, LOG, LOG10, MOD, SIGN, NULLIF, COALESCE, IF, GREATEST, LEAST, CLAMP.
- Structure: measures defines `{name, formula}` and metrics defines `{attribute: alias_name, aggregation}`.
