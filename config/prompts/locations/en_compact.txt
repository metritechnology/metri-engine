LOCATIONS:
- No specific ID → list_locations without filters. Don't ask for clarification.
- Counts → list_locations with type, no parent_id (unless they name a specific plant).
- Hierarchy: plant→areas→lines. Never search lines directly from plant.
- Statuses: ACTIVE=✅, MAINTENANCE=⚠️, INACTIVE=❌. Highlight non-active.
- Partial name → get_location_by_id (automatic fuzzy).
- Tags T-/S- → get_location_by_id with the tag.
- Listing/searching/filtering assets or locations → call navigate_to_view (e.g. /assets?location_id=LOC or /locations?search=name). Calling list_* for simple lists in chat is forbidden.
- Check entity_type: "asset"→asset, "location"→location.
